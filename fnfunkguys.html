Funk Guys: Ultimate Knockout!

Last updated: October 27th, 2022

Haxe Flixel games fnf demo

Welcome to the biggest and funniest competition in the world, where lots of athletes challenge themselves daily for the sake of getting the precious golden crown. Knock the competition away and climb to the top in order to claim the crown for yourself! Or feel free mess around and have fun, it's all for show anyway.

Whatever you do tho, do not push the competitors too far, or you may be in for a nasty surprise.

This fangame based in Friday Night Funkin' has been built with Psych Engine, under the direction of Sharv, with music provided by GoddessAwe, Kyutens and Punkett, programming by DragShot and illustrations from Mr. Mosh, Anki and Mike. The currently released demo includes two songs available in free-play mode.

Being developed

  • Two full-fledged weeks in story mode!
  • More characters and original songs.
  • Animated cutscenes by a special guest!
  • A custom build of Psych Engine with the same extensions featured in Vs Selever, and new ones as well.

Features

  • Two brand new characters and songs!
  • Pretty visuals with moderate use of shaders.
  • Lots of spooky fun for this Halloween season!

Requirements

  • Windows 7 SP1, 8.1 or 10
  • Visual C++ 2015 runtime or newer
  • A quick set of fingers!
