Psych Engine XTended

Last updated: Sep 26th, 2023

Haxe Flixel engines fnf

Under development, This is a fork of Psych Engine 0.6.3 made for its use in the creation of games/mods based on Friday Night Funkin'. It includes all of the features of Psych Engine, plus the additions featured in Vs Selever 2.0 and new ones on their way; basically becoming the next step from the later project.

This engine is being prepared to be the foundation of upcoming games like Funk Guys, as well as other mods I might get involved later on. You are also invited to use this engine for your own mod if you are looking for a powerful alternative with no need for source modifications.

For more information, resources and code, you can check out its page on GitHub.

Being developed

  • Extend the reach of the localization system to images and other mod assets.
  • Some helper scripts to include as stock.
  • Visual editors.

Features

  • Incorporates fixes introduced after the release of Psych 0.6.3.
  • The ability to replace existing game menus and create completely new ones using Lua scripts!
  • Automatic loading of pre and post-song dialogues in Story mode.
  • Localization support for dialogues.
  • Minor extensions to the bitmap font used in menus and dialogues.
  • Many fixes and stability improvements for the Lua scripting engine.
  • Several new Lua functions.
  • Customizable intro text (loaded from your modfolder).

Requirements

  • Windows 7, 10 or later
  • Microsoft Visual C++ Runtine 2015 or later
  • Any Lua script editor of your choice.
